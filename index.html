About

Note: This website is primarily updated and organized with AI assistance, using content from my local notes and repositories. I have not thoroughly reviewed all of the content.

I am a junior undergraduate studying physics at Nanjing University. I am interested in experimental condensed matter physics, particularly two-dimensional quantum materials and the novel physical phenomena they host.

I am currently building my foundations in solid-state and condensed matter physics. I am also curious about how computation and AI can support experimental physics.
